4 www.vulcanvaluepartners.com || 205.803.1582 We did not purchase any new positions during the quarter. We sold two positions during the quarter: IQVIA Holdings Inc. and CarMax, Inc. There were four material contributors to performance: IQVIA Holdings Inc., ServiceNow, Inc., Microsoft Corporation, and SAP SE. There were no material detractors to performance. We sold IQVIA Holdings during the quarter. The company is the largest healthcare contract research organization (CRO) in the world. CROs are hired by sponsors ranging from large pharma to small biotech and medical device companies to support research, development and ultimately the commercialization of drugs, vaccines and devices. We purchased IQVIA in the summer of 2025 at a time when large pharma clients were re-prioritizing and pausing work in response to persistent inflation, higher rates, and policy uncertainty. At the same time, small biotech clients were experiencing a more difficult funding environment due to similar dynamics. Given our long-term time horizon, admiration for the business, and confidence in IQVIA’s management, we looked through these short-term headwinds and purchased the business at below 50 cents on the dollar of our estimate of intrinsic value. Over our ownership period, our value per share grew, supported by healthy earnings, billions of dollars of free cash flow, and management’s intelligent deployment of that cash. Fast forward to today and IQVIA’s customers are now spending at a more normal cadence, and revenue growth has accelerated. As a result, the company’s share price has risen and is now trading closer to our estimate of value per share. Given how attractive other opportunities are within our portfolio, we have exited IQVIA to fund more deeply discounted businesses. CarMax, a company which struggled operationally in 2025, was extremely discounted earlier this year. Recent operating results have improved since the changes in management. We think highly of the new CEO, Keith Barr, and note his long, successful tenure as CEO of InterContinental Hotels Group, another MVP company that we have owned successfully in the past. CarMax’s stock price has rallied strongly this year, and we sold it to allocate capital to more discounted names.
