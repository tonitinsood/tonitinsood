The main risk for AI companies is earnings' durability. When we bought Nvidia (NVDA $237.47) in early 2024 in the $50s, people questioned whether surging profits were sustainable. Its fiscal 2027 revenues of $410B, operating income of $269B, and free cash flow of $186B will be 7-8x higher than when durability questions first arose.
