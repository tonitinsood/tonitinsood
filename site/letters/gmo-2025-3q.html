Quick take

Introduction Most investors believe that the U.S. economy is superior to the rest of the world and that the S&P 500 is better than all other groups of stocks. 1 The common wisdom is that if you invest in U.S. equities “for the long term,” you’ll compound capital at somewhere between 9% and 12%. Going outside the U.S., by contrast, is generally seen as needlessly risky: returns in the rest of the world have been somewhere between awful (China) and uninspiring (Europe) since the GFC, and it is a fact that no economy today can boast of the resilience we have seen in post-pandemic America.
