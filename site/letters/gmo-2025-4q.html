Quick take

Executive Summary AI looks like a classic investment bubble to us, with very high valuations and signs of rampant speculation. But we recognize that while many investors harbor fears that AI might be a bubble, they are far from sure of that fact and tend to assume the market is appropriately priced as a fairly strong prior. In many bubbles, such a scenario would make building a strong portfolio close to impossible since the portfolio you’d hold if you believe in the bubble is a portfolio that would be crazy to hold if times were normal.
