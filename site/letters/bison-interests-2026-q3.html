Saturn Oil & Gas History Saturn is a Canadian oil producer operating primarily in Saskatchewan and Alberta. Approximately 80% of its production consists of oil and other liquids. Historically, large acquisitions, share dilution, and debt weighed on the stock. We first became involved in late 2021 and early 2022, engaging with management and the board to encourage a greater focus on developing existing assets and improving capital allocation. We added to our investment in late 2024 and early 2025 as operating results improved while the shares remained inexpensive.
