Meanwhile, second-quarter sales grew 25 percent year over year at constant currency, led by Nemluvio, Galderma’s treatment for inflammatory skin conditions. Nemluvio generated $248 million in quarterly sales, while neuromodulators and dermatological skin care also grew at double-digit rates. Taiwan Semiconductor (TSMC) shares fluctuated alongside semiconductor stocks more broadly, with a large July decline and a choppy recovery afterward. The AI ecosystem faced several debates over the course of the quarter, one of which was the emergence of open models as a credible alternative to closed models for many use cases. For TSMC, what matters most for our AI investment case is ongoing scaling progress and token growth driven by training leading-edge models. In our view, whether this is done by frontier labs or not should not matter for fabrication demand. The durability of the AI investment cycle more broadly also continues to drive sentiment for TSMC, but September's announced 10 percent price hike reflects continued ecosystem demand and the business's persistent pricing power. We continue to own the business at a large weight across portfolios. A deceleration in overall industry billings is one gauge we are monitoring and could be a headwind to TSMC's near-term investment results. That said, consensus estimates for TSMC's 2027 and 2028 earnings rose over the course of the quarter, so this is not yet a warning sign. Ajinomoto sells food, seasonings, specialty ingredients, biotechnology products, and functional materials used in advanced semiconductor packaging, all built on a common foundation in amino science. Shares declined despite fiscal first-quarter results that we viewed as strong, following share price appreciation earlier in the year. Group sales rose 13 percent year over year and business profit rose 27 percent, both ahead of consensus expectations. Functional Materials drove most of the upside, with sales up 54 percent and business profit up 77 percent as demand strengthened for higher-value Ajinomoto Build-up Film, an insulation material used in the packaging of most graphics processing units. Management raised its full-year forecast, and the revision came from Functional Materials. Food remained a steady source of earnings, while the smaller frozen foods business was weaker. In our view, the shares have continued to track sentiment toward AI and semiconductor demand more closely than the company's reported results.
