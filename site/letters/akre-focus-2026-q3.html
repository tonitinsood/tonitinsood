Our overall take is unchanged: we believe we own a portfolio of demonstrably outstanding businesses whose valuations are uncharacteristically low relative to their quality and future prospects. Arriving at this undervalued state has made for a rough road these past 15 months and counting. We believe the disconnect between fundamentals and valuations sets the stage for an exciting future that we look forward to sharing with our fellow shareholders. Let us ground ourselves in a few of our portfolio’s key statistics as of September 30: • Growth: Free cash flow per share 1 (FCFPS) grew 22% during Q2 and year -to-date2. We project mid-teens annualized FCFPS growth for the portfolio over the next five years3. • High Profitability and Returns on Capital: Free cash flow return on invested capital (ROIC) will be over 43% in 2026, per our estimates, higher than 2025’s 42%. • Strong Balance Sheets: Debt to trailing 12 -month EBITDA of just 1.54x vs. 2.13x for the S&P 500, per Bloomberg.
