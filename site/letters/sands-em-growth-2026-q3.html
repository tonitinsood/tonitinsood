September 30, 2026 Sands Capital Emerging Markets Growth Quarterly Report (USD) 8 CAT L is the world's largest lithium-ion battery manufacturer by market share. During the quarter, the business faced investor debates over margins, overcapacity, and sodium- ion competition, none of which we view as derailing the investment case. Gross profit per unit softened in the second quarter as CATL absorbed higher material costs to gain share while peers struggled. Unit net profit leads competitors by a wide margin, and the business continues to generate free cash flow. On overcapacity, we are not concerned yet. CATL has expanded capacity to 1,050 GWh annualized while running above 95 percent utilization with output largely sold out, evidence that capacity is tracking rather than outrunning demand. Growth drivers include electric-commercial-vehicle adoption, faster EV growth outside Europe and the United States, rising battery content per vehicle, and AI-data-center- driven storage demand. CATL also looks well positioned for a shift toward lower-cost, longer-life sodium-ion batteries. It launched the first commercially validated sodium-ion storage system in June and secured major orders, including a three- year, 60-GWh contract with Hyperstrong. The announced share buyback, the largest in A-share history, underscores management's confidence in the business. Portfolio Activity This quarter's changes to the portfolio continue to reflect our constructive outlook on the AI demand environment. Our recent research in Asia revealed continued strong demand across key segments such as compute, memory, and power, broadening to smaller parts of the value chain such as networking, packaging, printed circuit board materials, and components. Our work points to three key takeaways. First, companies across the value chain cannot meet current demand. Second, hyperscalers and other key customers are committing to long-term agreements that extend supplier revenue visibility beyond the next two to three years and include upfront cash payments to support suppliers' capacity expansion. Third, AI infrastructure businesses are experiencing difficulty within their own supply chains, leading some to seek alternative sources of supply on short notice or invest directly in critical areas to meet their commitments to customers. We completed the purchases of ASPEED Technology, Elite Material, Samsung Electro-Mechanics (SEMCO), Shanghai Conant Optical, and Zhongji Innolight. We completed the sales of Al Rajhi Bank, COSMAX, Full Truck Alliance, and HDFC Life Insurance. ASPEED Technology is the world’s largest designer of baseboard management controller (BMC) chips by market share. BMCs are critical components of cloud data centers, acting as the nervous systems of the servers to perform vital health checks, such as monitoring temperature, voltage, hardware errors, and security. BMCs proactively detect issues and can take corrective action without manual intervention. These chips reduce servers' downtime, save energy, prevent costly failures, and maintain stability, making them indispensable to modern data centers.
